# Map zoom hierarchy and database summaries — 9 November 2026

The city-community layer now gives records priority at street scale. At zoom 8 through below 13, city areas use a 1 px colored outline (1.6 px hovered, 1.8 px selected), a restrained white casing and very low fill opacity. At zoom 13 and above, all city fills/outlines and the entrance hover preview disappear, and a map-background click cannot open a city. At zoom below 8, city geometry is hidden and available records are summarized in circular province/metro markers. The keyboard city selector remains available at every scale. MapLibre and the raster fallback share these thresholds.

Province circles show a known record total, category counts and the geographic name. Choosing a circle fits its area. Totals follow the enabled category layers. Text, nearby, map-area and other record filters retain the existing point-cluster behavior instead of displaying incompatible nationwide totals. Active routes, manual location picking and a selected city's detail context retain priority. These are navigation summaries, not new province community entrances: the existing 149 whole-city rooms remain the only public geographic entrances.

The city drawer adds six circular summaries: animals, events, homes, shelters, groups and help requests. Safe public animal/shelter images are used when present; category icons are the fallback. Unknown counts are a dash, loading is explicit, and failed requests do not invent zero totals. The caption explains approximate/shared locations and unique animal counting.

## Data and service boundaries

The social API exposes `GET /api/v1/discovery/community-stats`, with a typed schema, real adapter and Next API allowlist entry. It counts full database querysets rather than the currently loaded/paginated markers. Animals use distinct published animal profiles, shelters/groups use published records, help uses open unexpired missions, and events use upcoming scheduled records with intentionally public venues. Existing model managers control fixture visibility. Only public image URLs can be returned; sensitive assets are excluded. Public results are cached for 30 seconds and refreshed by the client approximately each minute and after discovery changes.

Packaged city polygons are the same sourced 149-city geometry and stable UUID namespace as the housing/map service. `backend/social/data/community-cities-2023.geojson` retains that provenance. `community-provinces-2023.geojson` adds the matching source edition's 82 province polygons and Metro Manila outline, retaining upstream URLs and SHA-256 hashes. Independent city contexts without a matching province polygon use a city-sized navigation grouping. Province summaries can include published rural records; rural municipalities do not become city communities. This source edition is dated 2023, not a new boundary survey.

Homes remain private to the housing service. Authenticated `GET /api/homes/community-counts` reuses the directory visibility rules, blocking, invitations, account realm and opt-in map areas, without the directory's 500-result display cap. Counts use the chosen virtual-home neighborhood, never private street addresses. The current home-directory geography supports the existing three Davao neighborhoods; the feature does not fabricate homes elsewhere. Home results are not put in the public statistics cache. The client clears previous counts on identity changes and accepts only the matching housing viewer identity. Signed-out/unavailable counts stay unknown.

Only the changed shared map modules, types, new statistics module and bridge method were mirrored into the social worktree with its correct import aliases. No blanket sync, new fixtures, reward changes or unrelated game/profile edits were made. Both local backend services were reloaded; no migration is needed.
