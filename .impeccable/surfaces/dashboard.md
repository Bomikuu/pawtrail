OWN-WORLD: Cream ground, white surfaces, brown text, orange actions, teal help/status accents, rounded Shadcn controls, and beige rules; Nunito headings and Inter body. Animal imagery owns content space.

STORY: Choose personal or role-authorized work, recognize the relevant animal or contribution, complete the action, and understand its result.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Utility header and compact section/dashboard navigation surround a central task heading followed by real rows or labeled forms. The task’s primary action sits with its heading or form completion. Mobile exposes management destinations in the drawer and keeps one content column above fixed app navigation; optional context stays supplementary.

FORM: Grounded candidate 5, local community newspaper/journal, seed `0cbeff46`; preserve recognizable pet/person names and the approved identity system.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Pet identity remains stable across saved records, contributions, and management; explicit status and protected confirmation guide updates. Intent feedback respects reduced-motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
