# Adoption

Mode: Operate. Approved build path: code. Inherits the community journal in DESIGN.md.

Find a suitable companion, express interest responsibly, and submit or manage a private application. A1–A4 own matches, preferences, applications, and shelter review.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A companion’s story supports responsible interest; generic compatibility scores cannot replace a readable animal profile or honest application status.

OWN-WORLD: Cream ground, white surfaces, brown text, orange actions, teal help/status accents, rounded Shadcn controls, and beige rules; Nunito headings and Inter body. Animal imagery owns content space.

STORY: Recognize a companion, understand needs and optional rule-based matching, express interest, then complete a private application.

FIRST VIEWPORT: App shell frames a central animal photo/profile and readable needs, with the interest action beside the profile summary. Application routes replace this center with a focused labeled form and clear next step. Mobile stacks identity, details, and action above fixed navigation; optional context never carries essential information.

FORM: Grounded candidate 5, local community newspaper/journal, seed `0cbeff46`; preserve recognizable pet/person names and the approved identity system.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The same pet identity persists from discovery through interest/application; swiping has keyboard alternatives and completion never implies approval. Reduced-motion removes transitions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
