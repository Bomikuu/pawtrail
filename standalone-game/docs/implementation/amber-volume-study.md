# Amber body and hair volume trial

Open `/play?compare=amber-volume`, or choose **Fuller volume trial** from the
existing Amber model study. The left model is the approved Amber shoulder/head
version; the right model is a separate fuller variant.

The trial replaces the flat extruded bodice, waist, shorts and lower sleeves
with tapered elliptical sections. Fronts retain their existing artwork; side
and rear cloth surfaces have small nearest-neighbor seam/fold maps and discrete
painted shading. The jacket, cuffs and gloves gain a little depth. Rear crown
and goggle strap volume, fringe thickness and individual long hair layers are
expanded in depth. Hair retains its original sharp tips and strand maps.

The approved face meshes and face maps are unchanged, including all eye states.
The shoulder caps, feet, body scale, rig identities, action code, movement speed,
collision footprint, portrait and icon are unchanged. The flexible cape keeps
its existing deformation and is moved back slightly to clear the fuller torso.
Both comparison models use the same 128×160 frame, camera, integer zoom and
animation clock, with 64 pose samples per cycle and a 2.5-second idle breath.

This trial is not promoted into the actor factory, saved skins or game roster.
No new generated image assets or backend changes are included. Existing Amber,
Yae and weapon work remain independent.

## Review status

The owner accepted the fuller-volume visual direction and requested that it
become the actor skill's construction-quality baseline. The skill now records
rounded body sections, fuller layered hair and separate side/rear texture work,
adapted to each character rather than copying Amber's dimensions.

No automated checks, builds or browser verification were run by the assistant,
following the workspace instructions. The trial inherits the complete shared
pose implementation, but other action fits,
props, sounds/effects/shadows, portrait transitions and action return behavior
have not been visually reverified. Keep those pending before wider promotion.
