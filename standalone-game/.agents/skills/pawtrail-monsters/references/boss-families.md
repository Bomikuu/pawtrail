# Related boss families

The owner's full boss package includes the boss, **one mini-boss and three normal
mobs**. Backfill existing families only when requested. Inspect the roster first,
preserve stable IDs and approved art, and avoid duplicate members on later calls.

Give all four subordinates an obvious relationship to the parent through anatomy,
materials, palette, habitat or equipment. Design distinct silhouettes and roles;
shrinking the boss four times, recoloring one mob or showing artwork cards alone
does not implement this requirement. The mini-boss is visibly larger than normal
mobs against the unchanged player scale, while still subordinate to the boss.
Record world height and physical collision/hurt regions separately from pixels
and transparent sprite bounds.

Apply the same reference → matching portrait/icon/illustration → profile/stats →
native sprite or selected 3D model → animation → encounter sequence to each member.
Registered full-frame poses are valid native 2D models; name their actual authored
frames and interpolated motion honestly. Include idle, movement, attack windup,
contact/recovery, hurt and defeat. A hovering creature drifts rather than steps.
Keep alpha, complete weapons/wings/tails, stable proportions and native manifests.

Mini-bosses need their own named skills, not just more HP. Prefer at least two
different readable patterns, with explicit timing, cooldowns, actual hit areas,
damage and counterplay. Apply the boss's low-HP enrage workflow to the mini-boss,
including distinct matching art and changed mechanics. Normal mobs need a simpler
attack appropriate to their role; they do not automatically inherit boss enrage.
Profiles identify parent family, tier, origin, temperament, weakness, resistance,
HP/bar units, dimensions, available cues and local/server installation scope.

Use one family roster to link the parent and its four members in the existing
preview. Every member must load a real representation and playable encounter,
with accepted hits, contact sprites, health feedback, pause/reset and cleanup.
Only load the selected encounter unless a group fight was requested. Keep voice
generation subject to existing model/download permissions; missing dedicated
vocal cues stay pending rather than blocking approved combat work or shipping
silent files. Never convert a family preview into real community animal combat,
verified rewards or a live world spawn without its separate implementation.

Under authorized review, inspect every member's scale/art/pose registration and
representative mini-boss and normal-mob combat, including warning/contact,
weakness, enrage where applicable, defeat/reset and selection cleanup. Record
which members/behaviors were actually reviewed, screenshots, sizes and remaining
work. Preserve the owner's restrictions on agents, tests, builds and browsers.
